hello, welcome to our page !

Care4Mind is a website built for teens by teens, to aid them in their day to day experiences with mental health. In the age of modern technology and social media, teen girls are subjected to an all time high level of stress, anxiety, and depression. Various factors such as familial responsibilities, peer pressure, academics, and extracurriculars can cause teens to feel heavy emotions. This website is intended to be a one-stop-shop for all things related to mental health and self care for young girls. It will educate them on mental health, provide ways to perform self-care, offer a community of other teens to help users not feel alone, and provide links to other forms of media resources related to our cause. Our website is designed to give teenagers the helping hand they need, connecting them to different methods of management. Our website provides diverse social media accounts including podcasts, books, music, etc.

SELF CARE STATISTICS

In 2021, CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) conducted a survey about youth behavior. Their survey, Youth Risk Behavior, displayed startling results and trends among teenage girls. Nearly 3 in 5 teen girls, which amounts to about 57 percent of the female teenager population, said they felt "persistently sad or hopeless." That's the highest rate in a decade. Additionally, most data and studies conducted show that teen girls are facing significant mental health challenges. While mental health has declined for all teens, the decline for girls is much steeper than for boys on nearly every measure. This is why we created Care4Mind. It’s designed to help teenage girls have both easily and readily accessible resources to help them out in times of high stress and/or anxiety.

Care4Mind defines self-care as taking the time out of your day to do things that relax you, create a more balanced lifestyle, and improve both your physical and mental state. This can help you manage your stress in a healthier way, lower your risk of illness, and increase your energy + stamina. No matter how small or big, any and all acts of self-care in your daily life can have a big impact on your individual lifestyle. Dr. Matt Glowiak from Southern New Hampshire University states, “While there is no unit of measurement for self-care, I personally like to compare it to calories, which are literally units of energy. That is, one calorie equals one unit of energy. Here, I see one unit of self-care as a unit of personal fulfillment. The more units of fulfillment one has, the higher their life satisfaction.”
