RESOURCES

At Care4Mind, we believe that self-care comes in various different shapes and forms. This is why we created the resources page! We have compiled a list of different mediums, from books to podcasts and shows/movies, and even influencers that talk about mental health awareness and self-care prioritization to follow! This list will be ever-growing, with new additions periodically. Be sure to check in regularly! :) The Care4Mind team hopes that you find these resources helpful and that you love them. 💌 To connect with the rest of the Care4Mind community, use the button below to join our community Discord!

Books 📚

  • More Than A Body

    Struggling with body positvity, self love, and/or self objectification?



    If so, More Than Just A Body: Your Body is in Instrument Not an Ornament, by Lindsay Kite & Lexie Kite might be a good read for you, as it aids in the creation of healthy relationship with your body.





  • Atomic Habits

    Strugling with the creation of new habits to better any aspect of your life?



    If so, Atomic Habits, by Dr. Julie Smith might be a good read for you, as it offers advice the simple yet effective ways of creating habits to better your life.





  • I Thought it Was Just Me

    Struggling with feelings of shame and not being "enough"?



    If so, I Thought It Was Just Me by Brené Brown offers powerful strategies to reclaim relationships and turn shame into courage and compassion.





  • Elena Vanishing: A Memoir

    Struggling to find stories that reflect your experiences?



    If so, Elena Vanishing: A Memoir by Elena Dunkle and Clare B. Dunkle offers a poignant perspective on anorexia, narrated through Elena's harrowing journey, making it essential for anyone grappling with eating disorders.





  • The Neuroscience of Self-Love

    Do you struggle to understand your thoughts and moods as chemical reactions in your brain, and feel powerless to control them?



    If so, The Neuroscience of Self-Love by Alexis Fernandez-Preiksa provides practical tools rooted in neuroscience to rewire your brain for a more balanced self, fostering self-trust and genuine self-love through optimized decision-making and mindfulness exercises.




  • Why Has Nobody Told Me This Before?

    Strugling with anxiety, depression, and/or self critisim?



    If so, Why Has Nobody Told Me This Before?, by Dr. Julie Smith might be a good read for you, as it offers advice on how to manage anxiety, depression, and overcome setbacks.





Podcasts 🎙️

  • Her Best Self

    Do you find yourself being affected by the constant rumination of food, wondering when you will break free from obsessive thoughts and/or damaging behaviors?



    If so, Her Best Self podcast might be a helpful resource for you. Hosted by Lindsey Nichol, this podcast provides insights and strategies to break free from the control of disordered eating, offering a path to lasting freedom and self-love.





  • The Teen Life Coach

    Do you feel like you struggle to find yourself, deal with anxiety or depression, and/or constantly compare yourself to others?



    If so, The Teen Life Coach podcast by Sami Halvorsen offers guidance and support to overcome anxiety, depression, and comparison pressures, helping you build a more confident and authentic self.





  • Growing With The Flow

    Struggling with navigating the brink of adulthood, life transitions, and building a life you love?



    If so, Growing with the Flow by Nayna Florence explores these themes, drawing on her experiences to offer insights and tips for self-discovery amidst various challenges.


  • Beating BDD

    Struggling to find professional podcasts that discuss body image?



    If so, check out Beating BDD by the BDD Foundation. They share stories of hope and recovery.


  • Not Another Anxiety Show

    Struggling with anxiety, from occasional overwhelm to paralyzing panic?



    If so, Join Kelli Walker on Not Another Anxiety Show for practical tips, casual conversations, and deeper insights to help you move past its grip.


  • Laura Morel - Breaking Free from Disordered Eating ...

    Struggling to manage stress and your relationship with food?



    If so, check out Laura Morel's episode on 'What Would She Know' where they discuss their relationships with stress and food.

Influencers 👩‍💻

  • Megan Jayne Crabbe

    Do you struggle with your body image and how you view yourself?


    Then you should check out Megan Jayne Crabbe, an influencer who shows the reality of social media and helps her audience feel confident in their skin.





  • Tam Kaur

    Do you want to start building your confidence and motivation to become your highest self?



    Check out Tam Kaur. She posts a wide array of content, from building people skills to goal setting, and everything in between.




  • Spencer Barbosa

    Are you trying to work towards a more positive view of yourself? Feeling confident in your skin without comparing yourself to others?



    Spencer Barbosa is an influencer who keeps it real with her audience, by acknowledging the fact that not everyone is perfect and that those expectations are unrealistic.





  • Annie Long

    Stressed about high school, college? Working on your dream lifestyle and future goals?



    Check out Annie Long. She is a teen youtuber who helps other teen girls become successful and reach their goals, while also maintaining a balanced lifestyle.





  • Nyma Tang

    Do you struggle to see diversity in your content, or feel unseen?



    Nyma Tang is a beauty and fashion influencer and activist who speaks up about discrimination based on skin color.





Movies and TV 🎬

  • Inside Out

    Want to see the different emotions your brain is constantly battling with and how that can affect decision making?



    If so, Inside Out is great for you, it provides a perspective towards the complication of emotions, and showcases the effects of tramatic events on an individual, while still evoking a laugh or two from the audience.


  • The Edge of Seventeen

    Do you want to see a coming-of age, relatable movie?



    The Edge Of Seventeen covers a teen girl, Nadine, and the timeline of her relationships with her family and friends.





  • Legally Blonde

    Do you want to feel inspired and motivated about studying?



    Then you should watch Legally Blonde! The iconic Elle Woods breaks multiple stereotypes for girls, empowering the audience.





  • Everything Now

    Are you trying to learn more about eating disorders?



    Eating disorders aren’t discussed as much as they need to be, but this show has! The show follows the main character, Mia Polanco, who is released from hospital after a lengthy battle with anorexia and thrust back into the world.





  • Wonder

    Do you want a movie about family, friends, and community?



    Then watch Wonder, which covers the story of Auggie Pullman, who was born with facial differences, and becomes the most unlikely of heroes when he enters the local fifth grade.
