OUR MISSION

Hello! We are a group of four students from the Kode With Klossy Summer Program. We are passionate about mental health and self-care, and we are committed to providing resources and support for those who need it. We believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to live their lives to the fullest, and we are committed to making that a priority for all of our members. We are dedicated to providing a safe and supportive environment for those who need it, and we are always here to lend a hand in any way we can. Thank you for visiting our website, and we hope you find what you need here.
